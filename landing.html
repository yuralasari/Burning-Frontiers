01 / Weather

EL NIÑO /
CLIMATE CHANGE

El Niño is a recurring climate phenomenon associated with changes in Pacific Ocean temperatures and atmospheric circulation. In some tropical regions, it contributes to drought conditions that can intensify wildfires.

When climate becomes the explanation

But climate variability does not explain every ignition. Human activities, land conversion, and infrastructure can transform forests into landscapes more vulnerable to burning.
