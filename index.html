What this prototype does: displays real monthly ERA5 U/V wind components at 850 hPa (~1.5 km altitude). Arrow orientation shows the direction wind travels; arrow length is schematic. Data are spatially sampled for browser performance.

Next: add NASA FIRMS fire observations, CHIRPS rainfall anomalies, land-use and concessions; compare climate-only versus climate-plus-landscape models against observed fire locations.

Critical limitation: winds are not an ENSO index and cannot identify who started fires. Monthly means cannot reconstruct individual smoke plumes.
